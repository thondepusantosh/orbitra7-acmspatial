Good to know

FAQs

Will lunch be provided?

Yes. Lunch is provided for every registered participant on the event day.

Is transport arranged for participants?

No. Participants need to manage their own travel to and from the venue — the AI Seminar Hall, Dutt Block, SAHE. Plan to arrive before 9:00 AM so you don't miss the inauguration.

Which branches and years can register?

ORBITRA7 is open to students of all years (1st to 4th) from AIML, AIDS, IT, CSE, ECE, EEE, EIE, CIVIL and MECHANICAL.

When will the problem statements be released?

Problem statements will be revealed on 9th October 2026 at 2:00 PM, one day before the event, and will be publicly accessible right here on this website in the Problem Statements section. Registered teams will also be notified in the official WhatsApp group.
